The trust badge

Put this on your own product or docs. It tells your customers the actions your agents take are recorded as tamper-evident, signed evidence — and lets them verify it themselves, in their own browser, trusting no one. It is a static inline-SVG anchor with no callback to us.

Paste anywhere. The verifier deliberately does not fetch bundle URLs from a badge link: visitors download the bundle they intend to inspect and choose it locally, so a query string cannot make their browser contact an arbitrary host.

The badge is honest by construction: it does not assert anything itself — it links to a verifier the visitor runs. The claim is only ever as strong as the bundle the visitor checks.
